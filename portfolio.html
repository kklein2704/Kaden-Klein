Live Cases in Marketing

One of the most rewarding parts of my junior year coursework in Marketing 4950 was tackling real-world business challenges for active clients. Working directly with corporate partners allowed my teams to conduct deep field research, develop tailored strategic campaigns, and present concrete solutions.

Delta Dental of Iowa — Healthy Smiles Campaign

Delta Dental brought us a key challenge: find an effective way to expand their dental insurance outreach to small businesses. Over six weeks, my team gathered primary data through interviews with local small-business owners and employees. We used these insights to build the Healthy Smiles Campaign, focusing on improved client touchpoints and long-term retention. Our hard work paid off when our peers voted our project "Best in Class."

Bethany at Home — Strategic Expansion

Bethany at Home tasked us with building brand awareness among younger demographics while highlighting their full scope of services. During this five-week case, we centered our strategy on community trust, recommending partnerships with trusted healthcare professionals for direct patient referrals, as well as outreach to local businesses. The client judges awarded our team Runner-Up Honors for our approach.

Team Recognition

At the conclusion of the live cases course, my peers voted me "Most Valuable Teammate." It was an honor to be recognized for driving collaborative idea-sharing, actively listening to every team member, and ensuring everyone felt supported and on the same page.

Personal Selling & Role-Play Competitions

During my junior year in Foundation of Personal Selling, I participated in structured sales role-plays partnered with HNI. Through these client simulations, I earned a spot in the Top 5 rankings across the course.
